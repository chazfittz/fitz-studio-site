What Fitz Studio is

Fitz Studio is a personal, self-hosted software tool that its owner runs on their own computer to produce videos and publish them to channels and accounts the owner created and controls. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Consent to automated uploads

Fitz Studio acts on a YouTube channel only after its owner connects the channel and turns on automatic publishing for it. That is the owner's prior, specific, and express consent for Fitz Studio to perform these actions on that channel through YouTube API Services:

  • upload videos and Shorts, and schedule them to publish at set times;
  • set and update their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions, and disclosure settings;
  • switch a video's thumbnail between prepared variants to test which performs better;
  • update the title and thumbnail of an older video that is underperforming;
  • post comments on the channel's own videos;
  • update the channel's banner, description, and keywords.

The dashboard names the channel each action applies to, never changes a video's visibility except to publish it as scheduled, and lets the owner edit or undo any change. The consent is recorded with a timestamp and can be withdrawn at any time by switching publishing off or revoking access in Google Account settings.
