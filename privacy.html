Fitz Studio

Privacy Policy

Effective 6 October 2026

Who we are

Fitz Studio is a personal, self-hosted software tool. It is operated by one individual (the "owner"), runs only on the owner's own computer, and is used only by the owner to manage YouTube channels, TikTok accounts, and Instagram accounts that the owner created and controls. It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and has no sign-up.

Contact: chazaiteam@gmail.com

YouTube API Services

Fitz Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using Fitz Studio you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information we access

With the channel owner's authorization, Fitz Studio accesses:

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens for the owner's own YouTube channels.
  • The owner's channel details: channel ID, title, description, keywords, and branding.
  • Videos, thumbnails, captions, and metadata that Fitz Studio uploads to the owner's channels, and their processing and publishing status.
  • Comments on the owner's own videos, including the public text and display names of commenters, and comments the owner's channel posts.
  • YouTube Analytics and Reporting data for the owner's own channels: views, watch time, audience retention, impressions, click-through rate, traffic sources, audience geography, and revenue.
  • Public information about other channels and videos (titles, publish dates, view counts) used for topic research.

How we use it

  • To upload and schedule the owner's videos and Shorts, and to set their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions, and disclosure settings.
  • To post comments on the owner's own videos.
  • To show the owner how their channels and videos perform, and to choose future topics, titles, and thumbnails.
  • To collect topic requests that viewers leave in comments on the owner's videos.

Fitz Studio does not use any data for advertising, does not sell data, and does not build profiles of individual viewers.

Where it is stored

All data is stored in a database on the owner's own computer. OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest. Nothing is stored on servers operated by Fitz Studio, because there are none. Encrypted database backups are kept in storage controlled by the owner.

Who we share it with

Fitz Studio sends limited data to an AI model provider chosen by the owner, currently Anthropic (the Claude API), to write and review the owner's video scripts, titles, and descriptions, and to summarize analytics for the owner. This can include analytics figures for the owner's channels and the public text of comments on the owner's videos. Anthropic's handling of that data is described in Anthropic's Privacy Policy. No other data is shared with any third party, except as required by law.

Fitz Studio shows no advertising and no content served by third parties.

How long we keep it

  • OAuth tokens are kept only while the owner uses Fitz Studio for the purposes described here.
  • Analytics, reporting, and other statistics for the owner's own channels are kept as long as needed, and every 30 days Fitz Studio re-checks that its authorization is still valid and the videos still exist.
  • Other data obtained through YouTube API Services, including video details and comment text, is refreshed or deleted within 30 days.
  • Public data about other channels and videos is kept no longer than 30 days.
  • If authorization is revoked, or a token can no longer be refreshed, all data obtained through YouTube API Services is deleted within 7 days.
  • Deletion requests sent to the contact address are completed within 7 days. Deleting Fitz Studio's copies does not delete anything on YouTube.

How to revoke access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account's security settings at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. To ask for deletion of stored data, email the contact address above.

Cookies

This website sets no cookies. The Fitz Studio dashboard, which runs only on the owner's computer and private network, uses a single session cookie to keep the owner signed in.

Children

Fitz Studio is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from children.
